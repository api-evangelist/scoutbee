If the average person wasn’t sure what a supply chain disruption was before, the Great Toilet Paper Shortage of 2020 put the spotlight on it. We’re now four years on and the disruptions just keep on coming. As a procurement professional, you’re more acutely aware than most of the havoc that supply chain disruptions can wreak and you know how important it is to have a supply chain that’s resilient, flexible, and responsive.
